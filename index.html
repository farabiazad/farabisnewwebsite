Meet the photographer

Hi! I'm Farabi

I create knowing that once a moment passes–some of that information, that feeling, becomes lost. We can't recall it all in our heads or store all of that data somewhere on a hard drive.

That's why I live to document the things that we love and need to remember. As time passes, we and the world around us changes. Through my work, I hope to give you something that stands as witness to who and what you were. And I hope it makes you continue to love and remember.
